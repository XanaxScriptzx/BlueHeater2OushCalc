WHAT IS THIS
------------
An offline Roblox "Blue Heater 2" build planner. Pick a race,
archetype, weapon, armor and shield - the tool computes your
final PhysPOW, MagicPOW, CritRate, CritDMG, HP, MP, GuardPOW,
walk speed, sub-stat totals and per-skill damage at any level
up to 120. 41 weapon-locked archetypes (GlassCannon, Tank,
SpellSpam, Movement, Support, etc.) auto-pick your stats,
talents and skills.

HOW TO RUN (PC / Windows 10 or 11)
----------------------------------
1) Extract the whole folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents, etc).
2) Double-click "BlueHeater2-BuildCalc.bat".
3) The calculator opens in a standalone Edge/Chrome window.

No install. No admin rights. No internet needed after unzip.

If your browser blocks the .bat, right-click it -> Properties
-> Unblock -> OK, then double-click again.

Alternate launch: just double-click "index.html" to open in
your default browser (works the same, just not app-mode).
